Frequently Asked Questions

What is Snares Linux?

Snares Linux is a independant GNU/Linux distribution built from scratch. It has it's own package manager and repositories.

What init systems are supported?

Currently under development systemd is the tested and packaged init. But our goal will always be to give the freedom of choosing your init and as always every program can be installed manually.

Is Snares based on another distro?

No, it doesn't derive from Debian, Arch or anything else. Packages are built directly from upstream sources.

Which C library does Snares use?

glibc.

What release model does Snares use?

To provided the most stable system as possible we chose to have fixed stable releases.
